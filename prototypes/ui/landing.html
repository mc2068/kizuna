One engine. Three jobs.

A normal hotel buys power from STEG and burns gas for hot water. More than half of a power station’s fuel leaves as waste heat. Trigeneration catches that heat on your site and uses it.

Step 1

Gas in.

From the STEG gas network most coastal hotel zones already have.

Step 2

Power on site.

About 38% of the gas becomes electricity the hotel uses directly.3

Step 3

Heat, recovered.

Another 43% comes back as hot water for rooms, kitchen, laundry and the pool, all year.

Step 4

Heat becomes cold.

In summer, an absorption chiller turns spare heat into chilled water for air conditioning.
