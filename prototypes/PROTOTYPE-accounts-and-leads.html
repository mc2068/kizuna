[PRODUCT NAME] accounts and leads

Click through how a hotel saves a sizing result, signs in with a magic link, shares a project and requests a feasibility study, and how the team then moves that lead through the pipeline. Everything lives in memory. The sizing numbers are placeholders, not the real engine.

Questions this should settle before we build

  1. Where does the unsaved result live while the visitor goes to their inbox? Only in that browser, or carried with the sign-in link?
  2. Can a hotel request a study without an account?
  3. Does a lead keep a frozen copy of the numbers it was requested with, or follow the project as it changes?
  4. Which pipeline moves are legal (skip steps, reopen a lost lead, undo a win)?
  5. Who owns a new lead the moment it arrives?

Assumption: the design (Keynote) is already chosen, so this prototype tests the logic behind the pages, not their look.
